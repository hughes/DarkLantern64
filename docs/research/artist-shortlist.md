## Project-specific selection criteria

The project is a first-person stealth game for actual N64 hardware. Its [vision](../vision.md) prioritizes legible light, sound, materials and guard behavior. The [current texture pipeline](../texture-pipeline.md) uses RGBA16, with small power-of-two textures and a 4 KiB per-texture limit; indexed CI4 palettes remain future work. Limited-palette painting is still a useful artistic skill, independently of storage format.

The [guard prototype](../guard-animation-prototype.md) uses 20 bones, five Actions, a 32×32 atlas, and one full-strength bone assignment per vertex. The current exporter caps rigs at 32 bones and is tailored to the existing guard. These are current integration constraints, not an assertion that every future character must duplicate that prototype. Evaluate delivered art at the game's native resolution and under its actual lighting. A modern retro-style portfolio does not establish N64 production experience.

### Proposed production pilots

1. **Concept:** One street/interior mood sheet, three guard silhouettes and a compact material/color guide. Start with Alix's work as the thematic reference.
2. **Props/textures:** A door, wall lantern and three loot objects sharing a deliberately tiny atlas. Review Zafio, Raildo, Taj and Bruni for the relevant strengths.
3. **Animation:** Improve idle/walk/turn/notice on the supplied guard rig, keeping its export constraints and foot-contact markers. Review Tom and Zafio; use Quaternius as a clip-library reference.

Ask for editable sources and a small paid pilot, then judge the exported result inside the actual game. Separate aesthetic match, demonstrated technique and production compatibility. No artist in this list has been verified as experienced with DarkLantern64's custom N64 pipeline.
